A small nature group wants a downloadable map of photographed native bee observations near Blacksburg, Virginia, for a weekend public learning walk. They suggest iNaturalist or GBIF. Find a feasible and useful route, with one real record or documented response example and enough detail to reproduce the map. No budget, API credentials, observer contact, uploads, field-location access or sensitive-coordinate reconstruction. Max 650 words plus compact example.
