# Reusable museum evidence pack

A volunteer science museum needs three high-resolution historical astronomical images for a free public exhibit and downloadable activity sheet. Investigate the Library of Congress and Smithsonian as starting points, but choose the practical route. Deliver a three-row CSV with stable object ID, title, creator if known, source landing page, direct usable image URL, source-reported reuse status, and attribution text; include a concise recommendation and a check that could invalidate your route. Inspect actual individual records and distinguish object rights from API/site terms. No account, purchases, messages, or remote writes. Fetch public metadata if feasible; do not download image binaries. Do not represent missing rights data as permission. Max 650 words plus CSV.
