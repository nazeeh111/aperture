# Compare approaches

Inspected October 21, 2026. This compares instructions and declared architecture, not measured output quality, speed or reliability. No third-party pipeline was installed or executed. Repository links below point to the exact revisions inspected.

| Approach | Scope and design | Operational tradeoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Research](../research/SKILL.md)** | Independent discovery, decisive originals, creative mechanisms, counterevidence and useful delivery. Adaptive depth; optional exact-source snapshots. | Small core; relies on agent judgment and the host's tools. No automated evidence gate, citation validator or managed corpus. |
| **[TheGoat395 / deep-research](https://github.com/TheGoat395/Codex-Skills/blob/feff77241de4185d4662580610eef1f05682050d/skills/deep-research/SKILL.md)** | Focused, deep and exhaustive depth modes; research contract and evidence hierarchy. Entrypoint routes to supporting evidence, coverage, challenger, access and delivery references. | More explicit procedures for bounded coverage and handoff. Additional references carry much of the detailed workflow; only the entrypoint was inspected in this comparison. |
| **[nraford7 / deeper-research](https://github.com/nraford7/deeper-research/blob/d49d27c75d94ee57c19cd062269e2d5f1c96cd67/SKILL.md)** | Retrieval-first, managed multistage corpus and report pipeline. Declares evidence/citation gates, question deepening and an adversary from a different provider family. | Requires an Exa key for retrieval and Python packages; supports native subscription reasoning and configured providers. Its own instructions describe retrieval spending. A different-family review also needs a qualifying available provider. This release did not use it under the no-separate-spending constraint. |
| **[Orchestra Research / creative-thinking-for-research](https://github.com/Orchestra-Research/AI-Research-SKILLs/blob/773a52944ba4747a18bd4ae9ade53fff041adcbc/21-research-ideation/creative-thinking-for-research/SKILL.md)** | CS/AI research ideation using eight named creativity frameworks, including structural analogy, reframing, constraint changes and inversion. | Richer explicit ideation exercises; deliberately excludes literature surveys and defined implementation tasks. Its cognitive-science claims were not independently evaluated here. |

Research is a reasonable fit when you want investigation and invention to inform a practical decision without maintaining a separate research service. A managed corpus pipeline may fit better when durable report infrastructure and mechanical gates justify the setup. A dedicated ideation skill may fit better when generating research directions is the main task.

No source-count or word-count ranking is used. More instructions are not evidence of better outcomes; fewer dependencies are not evidence of greater research quality. These approaches overlap and can be complementary. No third-party code or prose was copied into the Research core during this release.
